What is Phishing?

Phishing is a social engineering technique used by attackers to trick employees into:

  • Revealing sensitive information (passwords, personal data)
  • Downloading malicious attachments
  • Visiting fraudulent websites
  • Transferring money or company funds

Why Do We Test This?

Regular phishing simulations help us:

  • Identify employees who need additional training
  • Improve our overall security posture
  • Reduce the risk of real phishing attacks
  • Create a culture of security awareness

🛡️ How to Protect Yourself

  • Verify the sender: Check the email address carefully. Hover over links to see the real URL.
  • Look for red flags: Urgent language, threats, requests for passwords, suspicious attachments
  • Never click unknown links: Go directly to the website by typing the URL in your browser
  • Check for HTTPS: Legitimate sites use secure connections (https://, not http://)
  • When in doubt, ask IT: Contact the IT Security team before clicking suspicious links
  • Enable multi-factor authentication: This adds an extra layer of security to your accounts
  • Keep software updated: Ensure your browser and antivirus are up to date

Red Flags in This Test

This phishing email had several warning signs:

  • Requested immediate action (sense of urgency)
  • Generic greeting ("Hello Divine" instead of personalized)
  • Suspicious URL or sender address
  • Requested sensitive information (username and password)
  • Professional-looking but fake login page
